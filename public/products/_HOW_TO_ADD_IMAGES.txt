HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL PRODUCT IMAGES
====================================

You have 4 photos per product. Name each file:  <product number>-<1..4>

  Photo -1  = MAIN image (shown on the product card + main product image)
  Photo -2, -3, -4 = the gallery thumbnails on the product page

Example, for product 1 (wooden tableware set):
  1-1.jpg   1-2.jpg   1-3.jpg   1-4.jpg

Steps:
1. Save the 4 photos of each product from Temu.
2. Name them <number>-1 ... <number>-4 using the numbered list below.
3. Put all files in THIS folder (public/products/).
4. Tell me "images are in" and I'll wire them up.

Any of these extensions work: .jpg  .png  .webp

You don't need all 4 for every product — even just the -1 photo is enough.
Skip any product entirely and I'll leave a placeholder for it.
